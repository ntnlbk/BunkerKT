A team of scientists managed to bring dinosaurs back to life. However, the situation quickly spiraled out of control. Prehistoric monsters escaped and began reproducing. Herds of dinosaurs devour all the food they can find — vegetation, animals, and humans. City infrastructure has been destroyed. You must secure your own food supply and avoid becoming food for the new rulers of the world. Remaining population: 8,100,000 people.
Following a series of underground explosions, toxic fog began rising from the depths of the Earth. It causes people to experience vivid hallucinations and completely lose their sense of direction within minutes. On the surface, it is almost impossible to distinguish reality from illusion, and some survivors claim to hear the voices of the dead, walking straight into danger. Remaining living population: 1,100,000 people.
Zombie Virus — The progressive spread of an infectious disease among humans over time and across geographical areas, capable of triggering a major emergency. People have now become zombies. Remaining population: 1,600,000 people.
The "H" virus has spiraled out of control, turning almost all plants on Earth into aggressive monsters. Cities have become uninhabitable, and the destruction is global. You must fight off the monsters and find food. Remaining population: 780,000 people.
An anomalous evolutionary leap has begun on the planet. All wildlife has become deadly to humans. Invisible radiation emitted by plants and trees drives people insane — some are compelled to commit suicide, while others are driven to kill everyone they encounter. Once you reach the surface, you must find and destroy the epicenter of this anomaly while maintaining your sanity. Remaining population: 8,300,000 people.
A massive cosmic energy event — a gamma-ray burst — occurred near Earth. It lasted only a few seconds, but during that time it destroyed most of the ozone layer. UV levels on the surface have become lethal to all living things. Going outside during the day means receiving a fatal dose of radiation. Increased background radiation also causes various diseases. Remaining population: 2,500,000 people.
At the secret scientific facility "Isis", researchers were working on Project "Shortage" — a nano-virus capable of regenerating any tissue and absorbing pollutants from the atmosphere. The scientists wanted to heal the planet. However, during testing, the virus mutated, spiraled out of control, and literally reprogrammed carbon-based life, mixing DNA and creating hybrid organisms. The facility was destroyed, and the surrounding world was engulfed by "The Mist" — a dense fog consisting of trillions of nanobots carrying the virus.
Demon Invasion — Dark forces from another realm have decided to attack humanity. Almost all cities have been destroyed, but people managed to take shelter in specially equipped bunkers. The soil is no longer suitable for growing crops without additional treatment. Remaining population: 3,000,000 people.
A meteor shower brought something other than solid rock to Earth — aggressive extraterrestrial life in the form of a thick, semi-intelligent, jelly-like substance. This slime enters living organisms through mucous membranes, respiratory tracts, or wounds in order to survive and reproduce. It took over humans, using them as spacesuits to survive, and completely transformed them into predatory creatures. The attack was so sudden and rapid that many inhabitants may have ended up trapped inside bunkers alongside the infected.
Deep beneath the Arctic ice, a drilling team discovered a perfectly preserved, unknown creature. During an attempt to transport and study it, the creature thawed out and awoke from its slumber, causing a bloody massacre at the research base. However, the real threat emerged later: it can perfectly imitate the appearance, memories, and behavior of the people it kills, impersonating them flawlessly. Infected camps and cities collapsed due to total distrust and internal sabotage, as no one could distinguish friend from foe. The creatures began slowly reproducing and taking over territories. Remaining population: 2,500,000 people.
The world changed overnight. Evolution has gone horribly wrong: every animal now hunts humans with a single goal — to devour them. And the plants... they do not attack the body, but destroy the mind. Their invisible radiation causes insanity and an irresistible urge to end one's own life. The bunker is your only shelter during the most dangerous hours. Remaining population: 2,500,000 people.
A deadly virus of unknown origin is rapidly spreading across the planet. The infected lose their human appearance — blood pours from their eyes and mouths, their consciousness shuts down, and they transform into aggressive zombies that attack everyone who has not yet been infected. Remaining population: 3,400,000 people.
Blind predatory creatures with extraordinarily acute hearing have arrived on Earth. Any loud noise can attract them within minutes. Most cities have been abandoned, and the remaining survivors are hiding in soundproof bunkers and small settlements. Remaining living population: 1,300,000 people.
